“Days Without” Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Other languages

Days Without is an Android app that counts days without a bad habit. It is developed by xtreme_ as an individual.

In short

Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app does not connect to the internet, collects no analytics, shows no ads and shares nothing with anyone.

What data is stored

  • Counters: name, emoji, color, chosen preset, “my reason”, daily spending and daily amount if you entered them.
  • Attempts: start and relapse date and time, time zone, a relapse note if you wrote one, and reached milestones.
  • Settings: app theme and whether onboarding was completed.

This data lives in the app’s storage on your device. The developer has no access to it.

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your backup and restore it on a new device. This is controlled by Google and your Android settings, not by the app; the developer cannot see these backups. You can turn it off in Android Settings → System → Backup.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request access to the internet, contacts, location, camera or files.
  • It uses no analytics, trackers or advertising IDs.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.

Deleting data

Deleting a counter in the app removes it with its whole history. Uninstalling the app or using “Clear data” in Android settings removes everything at once.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently (for example, purchases or sync), this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change.
